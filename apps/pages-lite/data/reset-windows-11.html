  1. 0

    Before you start

    10 minutes, longer if your backup is big

  2. 1

    Restart into recovery

    1 minute

    Save your work. Press Win + R, type this, press Enter.

    shutdown /r /o /t 0
    • /r restart
    • /o open recovery options
    • /t 0 no delay

    The PC restarts right away and opens a blue screen.

    Prefer Settings? Open Settings, then System, Recovery, Reset PC. That route starts at step 4.

  3. 2

    Choose an option

    10 seconds
  4. 3

    Troubleshoot

    10 seconds
  5. 4

    Choose what to keep

    1 minute to decide
    Keeping the PC and your documents. Apps and settings still go.
    Keep my files
    Selling it, giving it away, or starting from zero.
    Remove everything

    Next it may ask you to pick your account and enter its password, or type the BitLocker key from prep.

  6. 5

    Reinstall from where?

    1 minute to decide
    Windows was crashing or acting broken. Needs internet and several GB.
    Cloud download
    Internet is slow or metered. Uses the Windows files already on the PC.
    Local reinstall
  7. 6

    Drives and cleaning

    1 minute. Only shows up after Remove everything.

    These can appear as their own screens, or under Additional settings, then Change settings.

    Which drives?

    Almost always.
    Only the Windows drive
    Only if you want every other drive wiped too.
    All drives

    Clean the drive too?

    You're keeping the PC. Faster.
    Just remove my files
    You're selling or giving away the PC. Adds hours.
    Fully clean the drive

    Fully clean may show as Clean data: Yes. It makes deleted files harder to recover. It is not certified data wiping.

  8. 7

    Ready to reset this PC

    30 seconds to read

    Last screen before anything is erased

    • Read the summary. Check keep or remove, which drives, and cleaning.
    • Anything it lists for removal must already be backed up.
    • Wrong choice? Click Cancel and start again from step 1.
  9. ✓

    Let Windows finish

    30 minutes to a few hours. Cleaning or a slow download takes longer.
    1. Several restartsNormal. Leave it alone.
    2. A black screen for 15+ minutesAlso normal. It has not failed.
    3. Setup screens: region, keyboard, network, accountYou'll see these after Remove everything. The reset is done.

    Keep the charger in. Don't hold the power button.

    Giving it away

    1. Stop at the first setup screen.
    2. Turn it off there. The new owner does setup.

    Keeping it

    1. Finish setup.
    2. Run Windows Update.
    3. Reinstall apps, restore your backup.
    4. Something not working? Open Device Manager.

Screens are simplified. Labels and order vary by Windows version and PC. Checked against Microsoft docs and the Windows Central guide on September 23, 2026.
