# A slower weekend

A small collection of ideas for a weekend with **less rushing** and more room to wander.

## Saturday

- Visit the farmers market.
- Pick a new coffee shop and bring a book.
- Take a long walk without a destination.

## Sunday

1. Make pancakes.
2. Water the plants.
3. Write down one good thing from the week.

> Leave some space in the plan for doing absolutely nothing.
